|----------|-------------|

| service → audit_log payload (extra='forbid') | malformed kwargs reject at Pydantic gate before INSERT |

| T-34-J | Tampering | concurrent cancel + cancel races increment past `session_count_snapshot` ceiling | mitigate | atomic_increment predicate `sessions_remaining < session_count_snapshot` is the ceiling; 0-row result raises RuntimeError (hard CHECK breach); CHECK constraint in 0014 migration is defence-in-depth |
