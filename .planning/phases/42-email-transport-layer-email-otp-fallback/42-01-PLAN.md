| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-42-01-01 | Tampering | email_send_log row INSERT | mitigate | CHECK constraint on `status IN ('sent','bounced','complained','delivered','rejected')` rejects bogus statuses at DB layer; downstream task fills `status` from typed `EmailSendResult.classification` only |

| T-42-01-04 | Denial of service | unbounded INSERT growth | accept | One row per send + one UPDATE per webhook; ~3× free-tier 2000/mo ceiling = ~600 rows/mo; growth bounded by provider quota |
</threat_model>
