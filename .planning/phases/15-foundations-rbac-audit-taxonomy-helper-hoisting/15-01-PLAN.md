| T-15-03 | I (Information disclosure) — reception accidentally sees finance/reports | RBAC enforcement at `Depends(require_permission(...))` callsites | accept | Out-of-scope for Phase 15: enforcement happens at route level (existing in v1.1, exercised by phase-specific endpoint tests in Phase 16/17).
