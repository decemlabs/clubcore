# Phase 999.5: client-pwa onboarding + post-payment receipt-email - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-05-31
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Two new `apps/client-pwa` screens, both persisted to the backend, matching the approved mockups:

1. **Newbie onboarding questionnaire** (`onboarding.html`) — 4 steps (имя → цель → рост/вес → проверка) + a «Готово!» overlay, with progress segments and slide transitions. Shown to new clients; finishes into the newbie Home built in Phase 999.3.
2. **Receipt-email surface** (`receipt-email.html`) — «Куда отправить чек?» with email field, domain-suggestion chips, 54-ФЗ reassurance note, «Отправить чек»/«Чек не нужен», success flash. Used as a **pre-checkout email gate** (the full mockup UI) and, after a successful payment, a **purely informational confirmation** of where the fiscal receipt went.

Backend additions in scope: new client-profile fields (`first_name` reuse for имя, `goal` enum, `height_cm`, `weight_kg`) with a client-portal write endpoint; an onboarding-completed flag; a rewrite of the online-payment email gate from "email required" to "email OR phone present"; and a write path for `client.email`.

**Visual contract:** reuse the existing `apps/client-pwa/src/styles.css` token system + `.btn`/`.card`/`.state` primitives — no hardcoded hex. The mockups share the 999.4 jade/stone palette; map mockup colors to `var(--token)`. Exact tokens/spacing/copy follow the mockups (and a `999.5-UI-SPEC.md` if `/gsd:ui-phase` is run); this CONTEXT captures the logic/wiring/scope decisions.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Email-timing contract (the central conflict — resolved)
The pre-existing tension: the backend raises `client_email_required_for_online_payment` BEFORE creating the ЮKassa payment (`apps/backend/app/modules/online_payments/service.py:104`, D-49-12), because the 54-ФЗ fiscal receipt is generated at payment time and ЮKassa needs a receipt contact in the payment-creation call. This conflicted with a "collect receipt email AFTER payment" screen, and with an onboarding flow that has no email field.

- **D-01:** Email is collected **up-front** (before the ЮKassa redirect), written to `client.email`, and ЮKassa sends the fiscal receipt itself. The post-payment receipt-email surface **confirms/shows** the destination — it does not collect-from-scratch after payment. *(NOTE: the "keep the gate unchanged / email mandatory" aspect of this decision is **superseded by D-10** — the gate is relaxed and email is optional. The up-front-collection + post-payment-confirmation shape still holds.)*
- **D-02:** The questionnaire stays **faithful to its mockup — no email field added.** Email is collected by a dedicated pre-checkout screen (the full `receipt-email` mockup UI), shown before the redirect when `client.email` is empty. The `receipt-email` mockup therefore has **two roles**: (a) the pre-checkout email gate (full field + chips + two buttons), and (b) a post-payment informational confirmation (success-flash only, no field — see D-09).
- **D-03:** When `client.email` is **already present** (set by admin or a prior gate), the pre-checkout email screen is **skipped** (straight to redirect). The post-payment confirmation screen is **always** shown after a confirmed `succeeded`.
- **D-10 (supersedes the gate-mandatory part of D-01):** **Email is OPTIONAL; the fiscal-receipt destination falls back to the client's phone (SMS).** Under 54-ФЗ the receipt may be sent to a phone instead of an email, and the client always has a phone (OTP login). The backend gate is **rewritten** from `email required` to `email OR phone present` — since phone is always present, the gate effectively never blocks online payment. On the pre-checkout screen: «Отправить чек» → collect email → save → receipt to **email**; «Чек не нужен» → no email collected → receipt to **phone (SMS)**. This keeps the mockup's two buttons honest and legal.
  - **Research dependency:** confirm the ЮKassa receipt object accepts `customer.phone` and how `app/integrations/yookassa/` + `app/modules/fiscal_receipts/` pass the receipt contact at payment creation. The gate rewrite and the «Чек не нужен» path both depend on this.

### Onboarding trigger & routing
- **D-04:** **Auto-redirect to the questionnaire on first login** for a client who is `membershipState === 'newbie'` with an empty profile, **plus** a manual re-entry path via the 999.3 newbie-Home onboarding-strip (the «Профиль» step / CTA). The routing mechanism (a new `/onboarding` route in `App.jsx`'s react-router tree vs an overlay) is **planner's discretion** — a new route is the obvious existing pattern.
- **D-05:** A **backend flag** (`onboarding_completed_at` timestamp, or equivalent status) marks the questionnaire as "do not auto-show again." **Both «Готово!» and «Пропустить» set this flag** → the auto-redirect won't fire again even if the profile is left empty. Survives device/browser change (chosen over a derived "profile non-empty" signal and over a localStorage-only dismissal).
- «Готово!» lands on the newbie Home (per ROADMAP — not re-litigated).

### Onboarding data model & persistence
- **D-06:** The single «Имя» field writes to **`client.first_name`** (overwrites the OTP placeholder / current value). `last_name` is not touched. No separate `display_name` field.
- **D-07:** «Цель» is a **strict enum** of the 4 mockup options (stable English codes, e.g. `lose_weight` / `gain_mass` / `tone` / `maintain`; Russian labels live in the PWA i18n / `ru.ts`). Not free text.
- **D-08:** **Single atomic `PATCH /client/me` on «Готово!»** — steps are held in local component state (as in the mockup) and written once at finish. **«Пропустить» sets only the onboarding-completed flag and writes no profile data** (chosen over per-step PATCH and over "save partial on skip").

### Receipt-email screen behavior
- **D-09:** The **post-payment** receipt surface is **purely informational**: «чек отправлен на {email|телефон}» + a «Хорошо»/«Открыть чек» action. **No email field, no editing** on the post-payment screen. (Editing/collection happens only on the pre-checkout gate, D-02.) This means the full `receipt-email` mockup (field + chips + two buttons) lives at the **gate**, while the post-payment confirmation reuses only the success-flash portion.
- **D-02 / D-10 (gate behavior, restated):** On the pre-checkout gate — «Отправить чек» collects + saves email then proceeds to checkout (button copy likely becomes «Продолжить к оплате» since payment hasn't happened yet — copy is discretion); «Чек не нужен» proceeds without email (receipt to phone). Email validation + domain-suggestion chips (gmail.com / yandex.ru / mail.ru / icloud.com) + the regex are **ported as-is from the mockup**.

### Claude's Discretion
- Routing mechanism for the questionnaire (`/onboarding` route vs overlay) — D-04.
- Exact copy, button labels (e.g. gate CTA «Отправить чек» → «Продолжить к оплате»), icons, spacing, animations, slide/transition timing — follow the mockups (+ `999.5-UI-SPEC.md` if generated).
- `height_cm` / `weight_kg` storage: nullable smallint with mockup validation bounds (рост 140–210, вес 40–150) and mockup defaults (175 / 70).
- Where the onboarding write path + email write path live on the backend (extend `client_portal` `service.py`/`router.py`/`schemas.py` vs a new sub-module) — respect import-linter boundaries.
- Whether the post-payment confirmation augments the existing 999.4 `PaymentSucceededView` (add a "receipt sent to {dest}" line) vs a separate component — see code_context.
- Onboarding `goal` enum exact code spelling; whether to persist the questionnaire `name` trim/maxlength (mockup uses maxlength 24).

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The user consistently prefers **honesty over mockup fidelity** where they conflict (carried from 999.3/999.4): no fake fields, UI reflects real backend state, don't promise what the backend can't do. The email-gate resolution embodies this — rather than fake a "post-payment collect" that 54-ФЗ forbids, email is collected up-front and the post-payment screen only **shows** the truth (D-09).
- The user reached for the **phone-receipt fallback** (D-10) specifically to keep the mockup's «Чек не нужен» button meaningful and legal — making email genuinely optional rather than removing the button or making it a dead-end. This is the load-bearing decision that reshapes the whole email contract.
- The questionnaire is deliberately kept **mockup-pure** (no email step grafted on, D-02) — email collection is a separate concern wired to checkout, not onboarding.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Re-sending / "second receipt" to an edited address** — explicitly NOT built (D-09): the post-payment screen is info-only. If a future need arises to re-send a receipt to a corrected email, that's a separate phase (would depend on ЮKassa receipts API support).
- **Editing the receipt destination after payment** — out of scope (D-09). Email can still be set/changed via the pre-checkout gate on a future payment, or by admin.
- **Per-step server persistence of onboarding** — rejected in favor of a single atomic submit (D-08). Resumable/partial onboarding is not a goal.
- **Richer profile fields** (фото upload from onboarding, birthday, gender via the questionnaire) — not in this phase; the questionnaire is the 4 mockup fields only.
- **Onboarding for lapsed/active members** (re-onboarding, goal re-survey) — only newbies auto-see it (D-04); a broader profile-refresh flow is a future idea.

None of these block the phase — they are intentionally outside its boundary.

</deferred>
