### WR-01 + WR-02: Validate `CLUBCORE_EMAIL_FROM` as `EmailStr`

**Files modified:** `apps/backend/app/core/config.py`
**Commit:** 31f98c40
**Applied fix:** Changed `clubcore_email_from: str | None` to `clubcore_email_from: EmailStr | None`. Single type-system change handles both findings:
- WR-01 (empty/whitespace guard): pydantic's `EmailStr` rejects `""` natively at construction.
