## Decisions Made

- `_customize_openapi()` is defined as a closure **inside** `create_app()` so `app` is in lexical scope. Plan allowed either module-level or inside `create_app()` — chose closure to keep the extension point self-contained without a module-level `app` variable.
- `refresh` endpoint included in `PUBLIC_ENDPOINT_OPERATION_IDS`: it reads the `sz_refresh` cookie but has no `Depends(get_current_user)` — it IS a pre-auth exchange and should be publicly reachable by callers with an expired access token.
- `type: ignore[method-assign]` is the only new suppression (FastAPI's documented extension pattern; no suppression-free alternative exists). Pre-existing `type: ignore[no-any-return]` on the yookassa client provider is unchanged.
