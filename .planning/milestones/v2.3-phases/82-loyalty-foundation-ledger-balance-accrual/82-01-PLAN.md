| T-82-02 | Elevation of Privilege | audit event registration | mitigate | INFRA-15: ("loyalty_accrued","loyalty") locked BEFORE callsite; AST gate (test_audit_taxonomy) + count-lock guards prevent silent taxonomy drift |
