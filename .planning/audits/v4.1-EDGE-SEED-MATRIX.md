## Notes

- **Error-family axis is mechanical, not seeded.** 122-05's manifest hunt and
  browser walk trigger 422/403/404/409/429 + anti-oracle cases against
  *live requests* using the fixtures above as the data substrate (e.g. the
  409 double-book race needs the `edge_empty_history_trainer`'s absence of
  slots to be a deliberate non-fixture -- there is nothing to double-book
  until 122-05 itself creates a slot at request time).
- **Structural N/A rows are a design choice, not a gap.** Domains marked
  N/A across every column (branches/settings, auth) reflect real product
  scope (single-gym, two-role RBAC) -- there is no edge to hide there.
- **DST literal-transition disclaimer:** Russia abolished DST in 2011
  (permanent MSK, UTC+3). No spring-forward/fall-back gap exists to seed.
  The `edge_dst_visit` fixture instead targets the real risk class in this
  codebase: UTC-stored timestamptz vs. MSK-derived calendar date disagreeing
  on which day a near-midnight event belongs to.
